# bots — Bot AI (`src/ai/bots.js`, `src/ai/bots/**`)
Rookie / Pro / Elite bots that play 5v5 FLUX TAG rounds like competent CS2 bots: they hear, see, remember, talk, aim like humans (reaction, flick + settle with overshoot, tracking noise, recoil control, counter-strafe before shooting), peek/strafe/retreat, buy (flow's `botBuy`), throw Haze/Strobe/Pulse from solved lineups, execute/hold/rotate/retake and plant/disarm with E-hold. They drive **the same movement function as the player** (`ctx.player.simulate(actor, cmd, dt)`), fire through combat's intent (`actor.ai.cmd`, `ctx.combat.switchTo/reload`) and read the world through `ctx.nav`, `ctx.map.nodes`, `ctx.combat.utility`.

## API (`ctx.ai`)
`createBot(team,name,difficulty) -> actor` (flow pushes it to `ctx.actors`), `removeBot(actor)`, `bots[]` (`{actor, ai, team}`), `enabled` (pause all brains), `reset()`.
Events emitted: `bot:callout {actor,team,kind:'enemy'|'execute'|'rotate'|'retake'|'push'|'util',text,pos,area}` (HUD/audio/tag-feed may show it), `ping {actor,pos,kind:'enemy'}` (only for bots on the human's team, ≤ 1 per 2.2 s). Bots read `tag:hit/out`, `footstep`, `weapon:fire/reload`, `land`, `round:*`, `actor:respawn`, `team:change`, `beacon:*`.
`actor.ai`: `{cmd (combat intent), diff, order, mem (Map enemyId → last-known), target, aim, fp (fire plan), mv (nav state), wi (weapon info), util (throw state)}`.

## How a bot plays
* **Perception** (`visHz` 6/10/14 Hz, staggered): enemy inside the cone (100°/104°/108°) or within 4 m, LOS from the bot eye to head *or* body (smoke-blocked via `nav.visible`), not blinded (`utility.blindAmount`). A sighting becomes a *target* only after the reaction window (`react` range × (1 + dist/90); ×0.55 if the crosshair is already within 7°, ×0.7 if the position was heard/called, ×0.6 if just shot by them). Blinks shorter than the window are never noticed. Targets beyond `engage` (30/42/55 m, less for pistols/SMGs, 130 m for Lance) are watched but not engaged.
* **Hearing**: running footsteps (22 m), landings, gunfire (70 m, Lance 110 m), reloads (10 m), plant/defuse sounds (28 m); range × difficulty `hear`, ×0.5 behind walls (`nav.visible` ray). Walking/crouching is silent. Position error σ = 1.2 m + 7 % of distance. Being hit reveals the attacker's position.
* **Memory / comms**: per-bot last-known (14 s). Spotting an enemy posts a team callout after 0.3–1.4 s (slower for worse tactics); a death calls the killer's position (0.5 s). Team intel feeds rotations, plant safety and defuse safety.
* **Aim**: spring toward the target point (ω 13/21/30 rad/s, ζ 0.5/0.68/0.78, velocity cap 380/900/1500 °/s) → flick with overshoot then settle; OU tracking noise in metres at the target (σ 0.40/0.17/0.075 m, ×(1 + enemy speed) ×(1 + own speed)), first flick of an acquisition lands with a 1.8× error; head vs chest chosen per 1.2 s window (`headP`); recoil compensation = `rcs` × the weapon's real spray pattern (read from `taggers[id].pattern`).
* **Fire discipline**: trigger only when the bullet line (view + pattern) is within `tolM` of the aim point **and** the weapon's inaccuracy cone is small enough (forces counter-strafe; rookies shoot anyway). Burst plans by weapon class/distance (rifle: 10–26 under 8 m, 4–9 to 14/18/22 m, 2–5 to 32 m, taps beyond; SMG sprays under 12 m; pistols tap; Lance scopes at >9 m and fires once). Recoil index must recover before long-range taps.
* **Movement**: fight cycle strafe → counter-strafe (`brake` against velocity) → burst → strafe; styles `strafe/hold(crouch)/rush/retreat(cover)`; nav path following with look-ahead corner cut, jump links, separation from teammates and per-bot goal offsets, stuck recovery (jump/side-step → repath → back-up → accept-near → snap to nearest node) and `y`-mismatch repaths. Never bhops; jumps only for nav jump links.
* **Utility**: Ember plan assigns each utility holder a lineup from the Tide anchors of the target site (Haze on the lane/anchor, Strobe popping 2 m above, Pulse on the anchor). Throwers stack at the first route node within range, solve with the real integrator, stand still, aim, press/release, then the team enters (`entryGo`). Tide retake throws Haze/Pulse on likely Ember post-plant spots and Strobe on the Beacon. Pulse is thrown on a defuser (counter-defuse). Weak/medium/strong power via `cmd.aim/fire/both`.
* **Tactics**: see team.js header. Ember pick A/B and style (`rush` 12–65 %, `default`, `split`, `late`), probe for map control at 12–26 s, execute at 30–60 s, lurker flanks the other site, carrier plants when someone has secured the site / nobody was seen for 5 s / time < 30 s (plant spot is checked against `beaconApi.siteAt`). Tide pick a 2-2-1 / 3-2-0 / … setup on hand-placed anchors, rotate when 2+ enemies were seen/called at a site, retake together (staging nodes → push → one defuser who goes when safe or at fuse < 13 s (9 s with Kit)), save at fuse < 20 s with ≤ 2 alive vs ≥ 3.
* **Buying**: delegated to `ctx.match.botBuy` (flow calls it during the buy phase). Pickups: bots without a primary walk to a dropped primary within 22 m when no enemy is known near it.

Overlay panel = per-bot state; lines: green = path, yellow/red = aim, grey = vision cone edges, purple = last-known memory, blue = utility target, red bar = stuck. `__game.advance(30)` to progress.
* Console: `ctx.ai.debug.draw(true|false)`, `.text()`, `.all()`, `.dump('Nova'|index)`, `.telemetry()`, `.rounds()`, `.intel('tide')`, `.team('ember')`, `.follow(i)` (spectate bot i POV), `.setDifficulty('elite','tide')`, `.tune('pro',{errM:0.2})`, `.startAllBots({difficulty,team})`, `.adoptLocal(d)`.
* Headless: `node tools/bot_test.mjs --seeds 1,2,3 --rounds 8 [--difficulty elite] [--ember pro --tide rookie] [--rich] [--log] [--quiet]` → per-seed round table (length, first contact, tags, plant), kills per weapon / distance bin, throws, stuck incidents, CPU ms/tick (×2 = ms per 60 fps frame), exceptions. Fails on: console errors, exceptions, < 95 % clean rounds, > 1.5 ms/frame.

## Tuning knobs
`bots/config.js`: `react, omega, zeta, maxVel, errM, errMove, rcs, headP, tolM, cs (counter-strafe), sprayDist, burstMul, tactics, util, hear, visHz, fov, strafe, peekSkill, crouchP, coverP, panic, engage`. Global `K` (hearing ranges, memory, stuck window). Team constants at the top of `team.js` (routes, anchors, staging nodes, setups).

## Known gaps
Bots do not use the radial ping, drop weapons for teammates, shoot through smoke at remembered positions (they spray known angles only when the target is briefly lost), or flash-avoid friendly Strobes. Lineups are solved from wherever the thrower stands (no jump-throws / run-throws), strobe pops are only approximate. Nav quality limits (ledge boxes near `a-ledge`, crowded chokes) still cause rare short stucks that the recovery ladder resolves. The player module leaves the human in free-camera after the first tag-out in a round (flow `setSpectate(null)`), unrelated to bots but visible in all-bot labs.

## Measured (round 1, `node tools/bot_test.mjs --seeds 1,2,3 --rounds 8 --rich`, Pro vs Pro, 23 rounds)
round length p10/p50/p90 = 44 / 86 / 122 s (CS2-like 20-115 s; armed rounds run longer), first target acquisition 18 s mean (8-56 s), 6.6 tags per round, Beacon planted in 61 % of rounds (retakes/disarms in ~25 %), win split Ember 12 / Tide 11, 17 throws per 8 rounds (Strobe / Haze / Pulse), **100 % of rounds without a stuck bot** (stuck seconds ≈ 25 per 80 bot-rounds, 0-2 snaps), 0 exceptions, **0.11 ms / fixed tick = 0.22-0.3 ms per 60 fps frame with 10 bots**. Sight-to-first-shot (median): Rookie 675 ms, Pro 360-450 ms, Elite 280 ms. Elite (Ember) vs Rookie (Tide): 13-3 rounds. Hit groups (Pro): head ~21 %, chest 27 %, arms 40 %; ~50 % of kills are crown hits (a crown tag is a one-tag kill). Kill distance bins 0-8 / 8-20 / 20-40 / 40+ m ≈ 7 / 40 / 40 / 10 %.

## Round 2 changes
* **Aim only follows noticed enemies.** Sightings inside the reaction window are private (`m.g`, no position/aim leak); only `hasSpot` memory, shots/hits heard (after `react[0]*0.8`) or team intel drive the look intent. Acquisition = static crosshair for `react` + `motor` (0.09/0.06/0.03 s) then flick with an angular landing error (`flickDeg` 5/2.8/1.4°) corrected as the OU noise decays. Measured crosshair error (Pro): 15° at first sight, 12° at 0.1 s, 11° at 0.2 s, 6.7° at 0.3 s, 4° at 0.4 s, 2.9° at 0.5 s.
* Accuracy/fights: larger noise and `tolM`, pistols rarely aim at the crown beyond 12 m, snipers/shotguns never; hit rate ~24 % (was 50 %); teammates in the line of fire block the trigger.
* Pacing: Ember `rush` 28 %, `stall` 8 %, default 29-44 s, late 50-62 s, lone carrier hides until time forces the plant; Tide forward angles (mid peek / long corner / tunnel) in ~35 % of rounds with a fall-back after 26-40 s; retake/save decided by nav ETA + disarm time vs `beacon.fuseLeft` (lone defenders go if they can make it).
* Quiet play: Pro/Elite shift-walk when sneaking (hold approach, lurk past mid, post-plant, retake push) and when they just heard something; ~55 % of moving samples at run speed (was 92 %).
* Callouts deduped per team (1 per 1.2 s, 1 per area per 4 s) with area, count, AWP/LMG, low; ~14 per round (was 26), `ping` only for the human's team.
* Spacing: convoy slow-down behind a teammate, goal offsets, separation. Planting: plant spots verified with `beaconApi.siteAt`, planter faces the farthest Tide entry (never a crate), plant aborts to a hold when a firefight is on at the site.
* `bot_test` CPU gate uses the median per-second tick cost (robust to machine load); telemetry adds callouts/round, run-speed share, sight-to-shot, dead bots shown as dead in the lab panel.
