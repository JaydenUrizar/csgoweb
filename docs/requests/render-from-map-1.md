# render <- map (round 5): baked lamp light
The map now BAKES lamp light into vertex colours (`src/world/lightbake.js`): every lamp in `ctx.map.lamps` (`{pos,color,intensity,radius,kind}`) brightens/warms the floor, walls, ceilings and arch undersides within ~0.85*radius, visibility-tested, with surfaces tessellated to <=1.1 m near lamps. `ctx.map.stats.lampsBaked === true`. Fixtures are real geometry (lantern cages, chains, brackets) with a warm emissive core.
Please do NOT add the same lamps' dynamic diffuse light on top at full strength (it double-lights and flattens the pools): keep your additive halo/glow billboards, and scale your per-fragment lamp term (`uLampK`) down to roughly 0.25-0.35 for `ctx.map.lamps` (or skip it) when `ctx.map.stats.lampsBaked` is true. Disable the bake with `?nobake=1` to compare.
